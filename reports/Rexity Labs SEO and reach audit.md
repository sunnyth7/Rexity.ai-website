# Rexity's Stale Google Entry Blocks Its 100K Path

Audit date: 2026-09-28. Sources: five research notes compiled the same day from a claude.ai cloud container whose egress proxy blocked every third-party host except web search (Google-style, US locale), github.com and the GitHub API. All price figures are EUR net unless a source publishes USD; "[ESTIMATE]" marks figures derived from benchmarks rather than observed.

## 1. Executive summary: Google mislabels the company and 100K ARR is a 9–15-month goal, not a 90-day one

The single most damaging finding is that the one search engine reachable from this environment indexes exactly one page of rexity.ai, the homepage, under the stale and wrong title **"REXITY.AI - Enterprise SAP AI Platform | Agentic AI Workflows & Intelligent Dashboards"** — a string that appears nowhere in the site repository's history back to 2026-07-23 ([Google: site:rexity.ai](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Arexity.ai); [first commit 6f72d11](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/commit/6f72d11)). Every AI answer engine on the list (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Bing Copilot, You.com, Phind, Brave, DuckDuckGo, Kagi, Exa, Felo, Komo, Lepton, Morphic, iAsk, Andi, Grok, Mistral, Claude.ai) and every classic engine other than Google (Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia, Yandex, Mojeek, Qwant, Yahoo) was refused by the organisation's egress proxy at the TLS CONNECT stage with a policy 403, so **no third-party AI engine answered and none is presented here as having answered**. The only engine that did respond, the WebSearch tool, knows Rexity Labs solely as the developer of the LevelKraft TELC/Goethe app ("over 5,000 downloads") and otherwise repeats the SAP-platform description; it found no LinkedIn page, no Handelsregister entry, no reviews, no directory listing and none of the 29 inner pages ([Google Play ai.rexity.telc](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.rexity.telc)). On-page the site is technically competent — self-canonicals, OG tags, Service/Offer/FAQ schema on every marketing page, consistent NAP, cookieless analytics — but it hides its best asset (six 1,800–2,400-word guides are noindexed and orphaned), ships three broken schema image URLs on its three most important local landing pages, and carries a 308 KB homepage with an 11.2 MB hero video ([sitemap.xml](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/sitemap.xml)). Commercially, Rexity sells 16 one-off fixed-price lines (999–5,999 € web, 4,900 € apps, 299–2,999 € automation) to a two-client reference base, with the only recurring item being "Jährliche Wartung ab 999 €/Jahr"; strict ARR today is therefore in the low single-digit thousands ([preise/index.html](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/preise/index.html)). The evidence supports one verdict: retire SaaS, dashboards, content, video and public QA/testing; quiet voice/RexFangs, standalone chatbots, RPA-branded automation and mobile apps; push booking websites for fitness studios and Kfz-Aufbereiter, WhatsApp reception and Local-SEO/GBP care, all repriced as monthly plans. A credible 90-day outcome is 8–15 recurring clients at 149–249 €/month, i.e. roughly 1.5–3.5K € MRR (18–42K € ARR run-rate) plus 15–30K € one-off project revenue **[ESTIMATE]**; 100K € ARR (8,333 € MRR, about 38 recurring clients in a mixed model) is a 9–15-month goal at 3–5 new recurring clients per month **[ESTIMATE]**.

## 3. Live-site SEO audit: indexation failure dominates a technically decent site

### 3.1 Method and snapshots

The live HTML could not be fetched, so the audit compares two repository snapshots: **main@d825e5c** (remote `origin/main`, 2026-09-28 17:33 CEST, "Mirror of the built site … merge to main = deploy", treated as presumed-live) and **local@15ec2b7** (2026-09-27 23:01 CEST) ([commit d825e5c](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/commit/d825e5cdcc2f10b989e62acba57ec48c0ebec9ec)). Between them, 213 files changed: every service page got a new keyword-led title and description, all 30 sitemap pages gained `og:site_name`, `og:locale` and a 1200×630 `og:image`, 16 service pages that had zero JSON-LD gained `Service` + `Offer` + `BreadcrumbList` (+ `FAQPage` on nine), `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` were added, and `/web/online-shop` was created ([compare view](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/compare/15ec2b7630f53a9888bc96a5bcce7081600554fe...d825e5cdcc2f10b989e62acba57ec48c0ebec9ec)). ### 3.2 Crawlability and technical baseline

robots.txt is `User-agent: * / Allow: /` with the sitemap declared and no Disallow lines at all, so the noindexed articles, the `/systems/*` stubs, the app policy pages and `/api/` are all crawlable ([robots.txt](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/robots.txt)). The sitemap lists 30 URLs in main@d825e5c (29 in local@15ec2b7, without `/web/online-shop`), but every `lastmod` was mass-reset to the build date 2026-09-28 (legal pages 2026-09-27), destroying a signal the previous snapshot carried correctly (dates back to 2026-06-15); 13 entries lack `priority` and none has `changefreq` ([sitemap.xml](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/sitemap.xml)). Twelve crawlable, non-noindexed files are missing from the sitemap (`/clevr/*` ×3, `/save-and-fresh/*` ×2, `/levelkraft-telc-prep-ai/privacypolicy`, `/privacy`, `/systems/*` ×4, the GSC verification file). ## 5. Strengths and weaknesses: real craft, no proof beyond two clients, no entity anchor

Rexity's strengths are concrete. It has a verifiable niche — "Website mit Online-Buchung" for local service businesses — with two live local references and three dedicated landing pages that say honestly what they cost (from 1,999 €) and how long they take (4–8 weeks) ([fitnessstudio-website](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/fitnessstudio-website/index.html)). The price list is unusually transparent for a German agency (16 published prices from 249 € to "ab 5.999 €", 89 €/h, payment in up to ten monthly instalments) and the copy refuses to over-promise ("Wir versprechen keine Rankings, keine Umsatzzahlen") ([niedersachsen/index.html](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/niedersachsen/index.html)). The team can demonstrably do organic growth: LevelKraft's page views rose roughly 4.4× from July to August after SEO work began and MRR grew from ca. $80 in June to ca. $290 in September, a case study most one-person studios cannot show ([work/levelkraft](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/work/levelkraft/index.html)). Technically the site is ahead of its peers: complete Service/Offer/FAQPage/BreadcrumbList schema, an OfferCatalog on the pricing page, per-page OG images, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` for AI crawlers, a cookieless analytics setup that needs no consent banner, strong security headers, and a working RAG chatbot with a curated, guard-railed knowledge base ([llms.txt](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/llms.txt)). Its hourly rate (89 €) sits at the DACH web-development median (90 €) with the overall freelance average at 103 €, and the 249 € Website-Check is a sensible paid entry product ([freelancermap Kompass](https://www.freelancermap.de/freelancer-kompass/einkommen)).

The weaknesses are structural rather than cosmetic. First, reach: no LinkedIn page in the index, no GBP visible, no reviews, no directory entries, no register page, no press, no backlinks; the two docs `GBP_SETUP.md` and `NAP_DIRECTORIES.md` (2026-09-23/24) show these were still to-do at audit time ([docs/GBP_SETUP.md](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/docs/GBP_SETUP.md)). Second, entity confusion: the only Google entry for the brand calls it an "Enterprise SAP AI Platform", the LevelKraft privacy page calls the operator "REXITY AI Solutions", the internal knowledge base positions "AI workspaces and automation for ambitious teams", and the site sells 999 € booking websites to gyms — four descriptions of one company, which any LLM engine will blend into nonsense. Third, offer sprawl: 16 service lines and three products in test against two paying references; nine of the 16 service pages (SaaS, Mobile, Dashboards, RPA, WhatsApp, Voice, Chatbots, Content, Video, Testing) have no client case study, no testimonial and in five cases no on-page price ([web/saas](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/web/saas/index.html)). Fourth, pricing that is 40–70 % below German benchmarks yet billed one-off — apps "ab 4.900 €" against every guide's 15,000–25,000 € MVP floor, maintenance "ab 999 €/Jahr" (≈83 €/month) against a 50–300 €/month market — so Rexity captures neither agency margin nor recurring revenue **[ESTIMATE]** ([TJ Labs app costs](https://tj-labs.de/blog-posts/wie-viel-kostet-es-eine-app-entwickeln-zu-lassen); [SL-SP maintenance](https://sl-sp.de/blog/webseiten-wartung-pflege-kosten/)). MwSt." contradiction, "Testphase" labels on RexFangs and RexDesk, a Kfz reference that is not live, five unpublished articles, and 14 fictional design cards outnumbering real projects seven to one. Sixth, the strongest external asset (LevelKraft) is branded LevelKraft, so it builds little brand equity for the agency name.

## 8. A 90-day plan toward 100K ARR, with an honest run-rate statement

**The honest statement first.** 100K € ARR equals 8,333 € MRR. Rexity's published recurring revenue today is "Jährliche Wartung ab 999 €/Jahr" (optional, ≈83 €/month per client) plus LevelKraft's ca. $290/month, i.e. roughly 3–5K € ARR in the strict sense **[ESTIMATE]** ([preise/index.html](https://github.com/sunnyth7/Rexity.ai-website/blob/main/preise/index.html)). Published benchmarks show how small agencies get there: an 18-year US agency listed for sale has 656 accounts at an average of $111/month (median $78.50); German Website-as-a-Service vendors charge 69–299 €/month; WhatsApp BSPs 79–149 €/month; SMB voice assistants 99–299 €/month; GBP/local-SEO care 150–600 €/month; and productised-service founders report 12–18 months to reach $10–14K MRR ([BizQuest listing](https://images.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/18-year-web-design-and-digital-marketing-agency-656-active-accounts/BW2461641); [webAION website mieten](https://webaion.de/website-mieten); [Indie Hackers 18 months](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/services/growing-a-productized-service-to-10k-mrr-in-18-months-with-no-audience-or-network-yUW5XGCZXfLJOolfun3e)). A mixed book that reaches 100K — 20 clients × 149 € (Website-Abo) + 12 × 249 € (Digitaler Empfang) + 6 × 399 € (Automation-Care) = 8,362 € MRR — needs 38 recurring clients **[ESTIMATE]**. At agency-services cold-email reply rates of 4–7 % and email-to-deal conversion around 0.2 %, that implies 2,000–4,000 outbound touches, and a one-to-three-person team that also delivers will not exceed 3–5 new recurring clients per month; 38 clients is therefore 9–15 months of work, not 90 days **[ESTIMATE]** ([Mailshake 2026](https://mailshake.com/blog/cold-email-benchmarks-2026/); [Belkins 2025](https://belkins.io/resources/b2b-cold-outreach-benchmarks)). A credible 90-day end state is 8–15 recurring clients at 149–249 €/month, i.e. roughly 1.5–3.5K € MRR (18–42K € ARR run-rate), plus 15–30K € of one-off project revenue **[ESTIMATE]**; a 100K € annual run-rate mixing projects and retainers is plausible sooner than 100K € of recurring revenue, and only if the fitness or Kfz vertical produces referral chains.

## Conclusion

The audit reframes Rexity's problem: it is not that the site is weak, but that the world has never seen it. A single stale Google entry, zero indexed inner pages, no third-party anchors and four conflicting self-descriptions mean every search engine and every LLM that grounds on the web will either call Rexity an SAP platform or an exam-prep app. Fixing that costs hours, not budget, and it precedes any selling — because the first thing a Celle gym owner does after a referral is search the name.

The second reframing is financial. Rexity has priced itself as the cheapest full-stack agency in Niedersachsen while billing everything once, which is the one combination that cannot compound into ARR. The market it already serves pays monthly — for booking software, for WhatsApp inboxes, for phone assistants, for Google-profile care — and Rexity's two real clients are exactly the businesses that buy those subscriptions. Narrowing the menu to booking websites plus a monthly reception-and-care plan, in two verticals within an hour's drive, is the only path on this evidence that reaches 100K € ARR at all; the honest timeline for it is the better part of a year, with 18–42K € of run-rate as the realistic 90-day checkpoint.

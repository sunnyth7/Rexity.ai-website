## KQ1. Inventory: what does rexity.ai currently market, at what price, to whom, with what proof?

### Takeaway
Rexity markets 16 service/landing pages plus 3 case studies in German, all sold as **one-off fixed prices** (999–5,999 EUR for web, 4,900 for apps, 499–2,999 for automation) with the only recurring product being "Jährliche Wartung ab 999 EUR/Jahr". Proof exists for exactly one vertical (local websites with booking: Body & Care live, Chara finished-but-not-live) and one SEO result on the company's own product (LevelKraft). Nine of the 16 service pages (SaaS, Mobile, Dashboards, RPA, WhatsApp, Voice, Chatbots, Content, Video, Testing) have **no client case study, no testimonial and no on-page price**.

### Inferences
- The catalogue is a "full-stack agency" menu (16 lines) carried by a 1–3 person team with two paying local clients and one own product; the breadth is not backed by proof and creates a positioning conflict between "Studio für Gründer/SaaS" and "IT-Dienstleister für Betriebe in Niedersachsen".
- Revenue model is 100% project-based; the only recurring line (Wartung 999/yr ≈ 83 EUR/month) sits at the very bottom of the German maintenance market (50–300 EUR/month, see KQ2), so even 20 maintenance clients would add only ~20K ARR.
- The public "Testphase" labels (RexFangs, RexDesk), the "Entwurf zur juristischen Prüfung" AEB, the not-yet-live Chara project and five unpublished articles all signal an unfinished launch to a visitor.

## KQ2. Demand, competition, price points and recurring potential per service line (Germany / Niedersachsen)

### Takeaway
Hard keyword-volume numbers could not be obtained (no keyword tool reachable; snippets contain only one agency's claim of ~4,480 monthly "Webdesign Hannover"-type searches). The price-guide and comparison evidence is clear, though: local websites/booking, WhatsApp automation, AI phone reception and local SEO all have established German SMB price points with **monthly** billing norms (69–300 EUR/month for WaaS, 79–149 for WhatsApp BSPs, 29–229 for voice assistants, 150–600 for GBP/local SEO), whereas SaaS/app development, dashboards, RPA, content, video and QA are either priced far above what Rexity charges (apps 15–25K MVP vs Rexity 4,900) or sold to buyers (dev teams, founders, marketing departments) that are not local Niedersachsen businesses.

## KQ3. How do small web/AI agencies actually reach ~100K ARR? Benchmarks and client × fee maths

### Takeaway
Published examples converge on two shapes: (a) many small subscriptions (a listed 18-year agency has 656 accounts at an average $111/month, median $78.50, >60 % under $100/month), or (b) 10–40 mid-ticket retainers ($300–800/month for AI voice, 150–300 EUR/month for WaaS/GBP care).
